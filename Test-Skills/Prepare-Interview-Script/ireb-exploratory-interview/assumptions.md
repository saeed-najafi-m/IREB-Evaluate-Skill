## Assumptions and decisions

**About the interview and the interviewee**
- **Sector unknown:** I don't know whether the interviewee works in a local-authority, private or voluntary-sector service. The questions work for all three, and an interviewer note says to record the sector if it comes up.
- **Tailored script:** The script is written for one frontline staff member, as the brief says, not as a general guide. Managers would need fewer walkthrough questions and more about organising the service, commissioning and resources.
- **Consent:** I assumed participants get an information sheet and consent form beforehand, as is usual in university research ethics. Q 1 still asks for consent again once the recording is running. If the person refuses recording, the script offers written notes instead. That fallback is my addition, so check it against your ethics approval.
- **Anonymity:** Staff will describe the people they support, so the opening asks them not to name them or give identifying details, and the walkthrough repeats this.
- **Wellbeing:** I added a short check on how the interviewee is feeling at the end, with a `[Support resources]` placeholder, because staff may talk about distressing cases. This is not in the brief.

**About questions and project fit**
- **Touch points:** To help the later analysis, the walkthrough (Q 8) asks directly for one moment that went well and one that could have gone better.
- **Optional Q 12:** This asks how the service collects and uses feedback from the people it supports. It isn't in the brief's staff topics, but it matches the project's wider aim of using people's experiences of care. It is Optional so it can be dropped if time runs short.
- **"Ideal system" question:** Q 14 asks how they would design loneliness support from scratch, followed by which problem that would solve, as the skill allows.
- **Last open question in Main:** The closing section must have no questions, so the "anything we haven't covered" question (Q 15) is the last item in Improvement Priorities.
- **Film and workshops:** The brief doesn't say whether staff interview extracts go into the film. The closing therefore only says that findings feed into the co-design workshops. Workshop participation is marked "[If applicable]".
- **Placeholders:** I invented no names or details. `[Interviewer name]`, `[Interviewee name]`, `[Employer]`, `[Consent form ID]`, `[Contact person]`, `[Contact details]`, `[Withdrawal deadline]` and `[Support resources]` need filling in.

**About timing and breaks**
- **No break:** 45 minutes is short enough to run without one. The opening tells the interviewee they can pause at any time.
- **Most time on the walkthrough and barriers:** These two sections (9 minutes each) are the core of the brief and the main source of touch points.
- **Opening and closing:** The opening gets 4 minutes because remote recording and consent need checking. The closing gets 3 minutes.
- **Tight timing:** The 11 required questions should fit if the interviewer keeps follow-ups short. If a section overruns, drop the optional questions (Q 7, Q 12, Q 15) first, then the less important follow-ups. Don't cut the walkthrough or barriers sections.
