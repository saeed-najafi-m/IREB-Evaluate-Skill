I've written the interview script for the GDS programme owner: 16 questions in 60 minutes, ending with agreement on the 2 main goals.

**Timing:** opening 5 min, context 7, each of the three goals 10, missing/overlapping/conflicting goals 8, prioritisation 7, thanks and next steps 3. That adds up to exactly 60, with no slack. If the goal discussions run long, the optional questions (Q 3, Q 5, Q 7, Q 9, Q 13) can be dropped. A second interview isn't needed for the planned scope.

**Assumptions and judgement calls:**
- **Goal list:** I took the three goals straight from the business-case targets in the brief and kept their wording close. There was only one goal list, so I didn't have to pick between lists.
- **2015 vs 2016:** G 1 appears in both business cases, so I treated it as one goal. Q 2 asks which business case applies today.
- **Results and current fit:** the script assumes nothing about whether the targets were met or still make sense. The context section uses only what's in the brief and leaves out actual results or the service's later history. The interviewer note tells the interviewer not to give performance figures.
- **Past dates:** the target dates (2020 and March 2018) may already have passed or no longer apply. So the follow-ups for G 1 and G 2 ask which target date applies now, and the one for G 3 asks by when the rate should be reached.
- **Unclear definitions:** the brief doesn't define "use Verify" or "connected", or say which people the success rate is counted against. I made these measurement follow-ups instead of choosing a definition.
- **Placeholders:** `[Name]` for the interviewee, `[number]` working days for sending the summary, and `[owner]` for open figures.
- **Closed questions:** Q 15 is a yes/no question about agreeing the main goals, and the confirm/reword/merge/split/dismiss follow-ups are choices. The skill allows closed questions for decisions.
- **Closing sections:** prioritisation (Q 14 to Q 16) is in the closing phase as the skill requires. A separate "Thanks and Next Steps" section without questions comes after it.
