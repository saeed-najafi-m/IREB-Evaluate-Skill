# Interview Script: GOV.UK Verify

Goal confirmation interview with [Name], GDS programme owner responsible for Verify's delivery and its adoption across government departments, Government Digital Service (Cabinet Office), to agree on 2 main goals in 60 minutes.

## Objective

Verify, update, or dismiss the three goals taken from Verify's business cases, find missing goals, and agree with the programme owner on the two main goals for GOV.UK Verify, each with a way to measure it.

## Completion Criteria

- Each of G 1 to G 3 has a recorded decision: confirmed, reworded, merged, split, or dismissed.
- Each goal that is not dismissed has a reason why it matters, a classification as goal or solution, and an agreed measure with a target value and date, or an open item naming who will supply them.
- Missing, overlapping, and conflicting goals are recorded, or the interviewee has stated that there are none.
- The interviewee has prioritized all remaining goals and agreed on exactly 2 main goals.
- The 2 main goals have been read back and confirmed by the interviewee.
- Open items have an owner and a date.

## Previously Collected Goals

Source: business-case targets for service and user take-up, as later reported (2015 and 2016 business cases).

| ID | Goal |
|---|---|
| G 1 | Number of users who have signed up to use Verify: 25 million people use Verify by 2020 (forecast in the 2015 and 2016 business cases). |
| G 2 | Number of government services that use Verify: 46 services connect to Verify by March 2018 (2016 business case). |
| G 3 | Verification success rate: 90% of people can sign up for a Verify user account having had their identities successfully confirmed by a provider (2015 business case). |

## Welcome and Purpose (Opening, 5 min)

> Thank you for taking the time today. We have one hour. The aim is to look together at the goals for GOV.UK Verify that were set out in the business cases, to check whether they are still the right ones, to find anything missing, and at the end to agree on the two goals that matter most. I will take notes, and you will receive a summary afterwards for review. Is that all right with you?

*Confirm the recording or note-taking arrangement. Do not mention current performance figures; the documents contain targets only, not results.*

- **Q 1** (Required): How would you describe your role in Verify's delivery and adoption today?
  - Who else decides on Verify's goals besides you?

## Context and Goal Overview (Main, 7 min)

> According to the reports we have, Verify's performance can be assessed against the expectations set out in the programme's business cases for service and user take-up. Three targets are named there: first, 25 million people using Verify by 2020, forecast in both the 2015 and the 2016 business case; second, 46 government services connected to Verify by March 2018, from the 2016 business case; and third, a verification success rate of 90%, from the 2015 business case. The success rate measures the proportion of people who can sign up for a Verify user account having had their identities successfully confirmed by a provider. I would like to go through each of these with you.

*Show the table of previously collected goals. Do not suggest whether the targets were met or are still appropriate.*

- **Q 2** (Required; goals: G 1, G 2, G 3): How do you understand these three goals as I have presented them?
  - If the interviewee's understanding differs from the wording: What would you change in the wording?
  - Which of the two business cases, 2015 or 2016, is the one that applies to Verify today?
- **Q 3** (Optional; goals: G 1, G 2, G 3): What has changed since the business cases were written that affects these goals?

## G 1: User Take-up (Main, 10 min)

- **Q 4** (Required; goals: G 1): What does the goal of 25 million people using Verify by 2020 mean to you?
  - Why does this number of users matter for the programme?
  - Is reaching this number of users a goal in itself, or a means to something else? If a means, to what?
  - Should this goal be confirmed, reworded, merged with another goal, split, or dismissed?
  - If reworded or split: How would you phrase it?
  - If the date 2020 has passed or no longer applies: Which target date applies instead?
  - If not dismissed: How should "use Verify" be measured - for example, as sign-ups, as active users, or otherwise?
  - If not dismissed: Who provides this figure, and how often?
- **Q 5** (Optional; goals: G 1): How did the business cases arrive at 25 million?

## G 2: Service Take-up (Main, 10 min)

- **Q 6** (Required; goals: G 2): What does the goal of 46 services connecting to Verify by March 2018 mean to you?
  - Why does the number of connected services matter for the programme?
  - Is connecting services a goal in itself, or a means to something else? If a means, to what?
  - Should this goal be confirmed, reworded, merged with another goal, split, or dismissed?
  - If reworded or split: How would you phrase it?
  - If the date March 2018 has passed or no longer applies: Which target date applies instead?
  - If not dismissed: When does a service count as "connected" to Verify?
  - If not dismissed: How should this goal be measured, and who provides the figure?
- **Q 7** (Optional; goals: G 2): What do government departments expect from Verify before they connect a service?

## G 3: Verification Success Rate (Main, 10 min)

- **Q 8** (Required; goals: G 3): What does the goal of a 90% verification success rate mean to you?
  - Why does the verification success rate matter for the programme?
  - Is this success rate a goal in itself, or a means to something else? If a means, to what?
  - Should this goal be confirmed, reworded, merged with another goal, split, or dismissed?
  - If reworded or split: How would you phrase it?
  - If not dismissed: Which people are counted in the base of the rate - everyone who starts, everyone who completes the process, or another group?
  - If not dismissed: By when should the rate be reached, and who measures it?
- **Q 9** (Optional; goals: G 3): What happens to people whose identity cannot be confirmed by a provider?

## Missing, Overlapping, and Conflicting Goals (Main, 8 min)

- **Q 10** (Required): Which goals for Verify are missing from this list?
  - If the interviewee names a goal: Why does it matter, and how would you measure it?
  - Which goals do departments, users, or identity providers have for Verify that we have not discussed?
- **Q 11** (Required; goals: G 1, G 2, G 3): Where do these goals overlap?
  - If an overlap is named: Which of the overlapping goals should remain?
- **Q 12** (Required; goals: G 1, G 2, G 3): Where do these goals conflict with each other?
  - If a conflict is named: How should the conflict be resolved, and who decides?
- **Q 13** (Optional): Which constraints, such as budget, policy, or security, limit what Verify can achieve?

## Prioritization and Main Goals (Closing, 7 min)

*List all goals that were not dismissed, including new ones, before asking. Read back the two main goals with their measures and wait for explicit confirmation.*

- **Q 14** (Required; goals: G 1, G 2, G 3): How would you rank the remaining goals, from most to least important?
  - Why is the top goal more important than the others?
- **Q 15** (Required; goals: G 1, G 2, G 3): Do you agree that these two goals are Verify's main goals?
  - If not: Which two goals should be the main goals instead?
- **Q 16** (Required): Who else must agree on these main goals?
  - If others must agree: How and by when should they be involved?

## Thanks and Next Steps (Closing, 3 min)

> Thank you very much for your time and openness. I will send you a summary of the decisions on each goal, the two main goals with their measures, and the open items within [number] working days for your review. If figures or target dates are still open, I will contact [owner] to obtain them. Where other people need to agree on the main goals, I will arrange that with them as discussed.
